Hear what you can’t see

Pick any image, chart or meme and AI Eyes describes it out loud in Hindi or English. Built for people who are blind or have low vision.

Try it now

Add an image, then press Describe aloud.

Your key is saved only in this browser. Get a free key at aistudio.google.com/apikey. Hindi voice needs a Hindi voice installed on your device.

Three steps, no setup for the listener

  1. Right-click an image

    Or press Alt+Shift+E to explain everything visible on the screen.

  2. AI looks closely

    Gemini Vision reads the chart, the joke or the page layout and writes a clear description.

  3. Listen

    The description is spoken aloud within seconds. Alt+Shift+S stops it.

Made for the things screen readers skip

Screen readers read text well. They struggle when meaning lives inside a picture.

  • Charts and graphsTitle, axes, trends and the numbers that matter.
  • MemesWhat is pictured and why it is funny.
  • Infographics and diagramsExplained in the order you would read them.
  • Page layoutsWhat is on the page, where it is and which buttons you can use.

Get the Chrome extension

Works on any website in Chrome on a laptop or desktop.

  1. Download and unzip the file.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Choose Load unpacked and select the unzipped folder.
  4. Paste your Gemini API key on the settings page and save.
